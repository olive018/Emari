Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater harvesting collects rain that falls on the roof and stores it so it can be used later instead of being lost as runoff.

How rainwater harvesting works

🌧️
Rainfall

Rain falls on the roof, which acts as the catchment area.

↓
⌂
Roof and pipes

Water runs to the edges and into the pipes along the roof.

↓
▤
Filter

Leaves and dirt are removed before the water is stored.

↓
💧
Storage tank

Clean water is stored for gardens, cleaning and flushing.

What affects performance?

Roof area, rainfall, how clean the roof is, and tank size all matter. As a rule of thumb, 1 mm of rain on 1 square metre of roof gives about 1 litre of water, before losses.
